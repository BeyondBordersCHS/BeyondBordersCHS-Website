About

Beyond Borders was founded in 2025 by Joshua Bahn, a current junior at Campolindo High School.

We teach English and help North Korean refugee college students adapt to their new life. We meet with them over Zoom once per week for an hour.

Our club meets during lunch on Wednesdays. Room TBD.
